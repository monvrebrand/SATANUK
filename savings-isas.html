Protecting your money

Your eligible deposits with Santander UK plc are protected up to a total of £85,000 by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS), the UK's deposit guarantee scheme.

Stop, Challenge, Protect

Stay safe from fraud – we'll never ask for your full PIN or password. If something feels wrong, stop and challenge it.
